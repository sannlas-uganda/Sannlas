What is SANNLAS?

SANNLAS is Uganda's #1 Reels Shopping App, based in Kampala, Uganda. SANNLAS is an online marketplace where you can buy, sell and request anything in Uganda.

Unlike Jumia or Jiji, SANNLAS combines short video Reels + Shopping + Requests + Transport + Manufacturing in ONE app.
