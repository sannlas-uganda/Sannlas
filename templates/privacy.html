1. Introduction

SANNLAS Uganda (“we,” “our,” “us”) operates the online marketplace available at https://sannlas.onrender.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

Your data is used to: Provide and improve marketplace services, process transactions, subscriptions, and withdrawals, enable secure login, facilitate live chat and support, and send promotional offers (with consent).

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures including Encrypted connections (SSL/HTTPS), secure password storage, and restricted access to sensitive data.

6. User Rights

You have the right to: Access your personal data, Correct inaccurate information, Request deletion of your account and data, Withdraw consent for promotional communications.

7. Cookies & Tracking

We may use cookies to improve functionality, analyze behavior, and store login sessions. You can disable cookies in browser settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
