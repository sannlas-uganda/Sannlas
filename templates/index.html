❓ FAQs with Answers
Q1: How do I sell on SANNLAS? ▼
A: Click + Sell button, enter Business Name, District, select Main Category → Sub Category will auto show, enter your private phone (only admin sees), choose plan 14 Days FREE, select photos, enter OWN PRICE for each photo, click UPLOAD. Your product goes live instantly!
Q2: How do I buy goods? ▼
A: Use top Search for goods, or open ☰ menu → Main Categories → click any main to see subs. Click product → View → Add to Cart → Checkout. You can also WhatsApp seller directly or Bargain price.
Q3: Is SANNLAS free? ▼
A: Yes! Listing for 14 days is 100% FREE. After 14 days, you can renew: 30 days UGX 6,540, 2 months UGX 13,090, 6 months UGX 39,500, 1 year UGX 80,000. Payment via MoMo 0795712326.
Q4: What payment methods? ▼
A: MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money to 0795712326, and Cash on Delivery with Boda Boda tracking. Secure checkout in cart.
Q5: How does delivery work? ▼
A: After order, seller packs and assigns Boda Boda. You can track Boda GPS in Orders + Boda section. Delivery within Kampala 2-4 hours, upcountry 24-48 hours.
Q6: How to filter by categories? ▼
A: Open ☰ hamburger → Main Categories → Click any main (e.g. Fashion & Clothing) → Its sub-categories appear inside it → Click sub to filter products. Buyer auto-filter works same.
Q7: How to follow a seller? ▼
A: On product card click + Follow. You will see follower count. Sellers with more followers get verified badge ✓.
Q8: What are Bargains? ▼
A: Click Bargain button on product, enter your offer price. Seller receives it and can accept. Good for best price!
Q9: How to contact SANNLAS owner? ▼
A: WhatsApp 0795712326 or Email natelieabigali@gmail.com. For NIN verification issues, go to My Account → NIN section.
Q10: Is my phone private? ▼
A: Yes! Phone you enter in Sell form (Your Phone - ADMIN ONLY SEES) is private. Only Admin sees it. Buyers contact via platform or WhatsApp link without seeing your private number.
Q11: How to become verified? ▼
A: Upload NIN Front and Back in My Account → NIN Verification. Admin verifies within 24h. You get ✓ Verified badge and more buyer trust.
Q12: What is trending? ▼
A: Trending Brands are most searched brands. Best Sellers are most ordered products. Deals are products below UGX 50,000 or on offer. Check hamburger menu.
